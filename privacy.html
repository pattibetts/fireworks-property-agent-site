Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Fireworks Property Agent is a personal automation built and run by a single individual for their own use. It is not a public product, and it does not act on behalf of, or have access to, any Gmail account other than the owner's own.

What it accesses

This app connects to one Gmail inbox, belonging to its owner, and:

  • Reads booking-confirmation emails from a small, fixed list of senders
  • Applies a Gmail label ("Processed") to emails it has already read

It does not read, modify, or delete any other email in the inbox, and it never sends email on the owner's behalf.

What it does with that data

Guest name, dates, and booking status extracted from each email are written to a single Google Sheet that only the owner can access. No other storage, logging, or analytics system receives this data.

What it shares

This app shares no data with any third party except the services listed below. The only outside services it talks to are the ones strictly required to do the above: Google's APIs (Gmail, Sheets) and an AI inference API (Fireworks AI) used solely to parse the structured booking fields out of an email's text.

Data retention and deletion

Data persists only as long as the owner's own Gmail labels and Google Sheet exist. Since the owner and the data subject are the same person, data can be deleted at any time by editing or deleting the Sheet, or by revoking this app's access from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pbettsenterprises@gmail.com.
